# S24 Work Term Report — BoscoBoys Distributors

Work term report for my Summer 2024 co-op placement as a **Fullstack Developer & Business Analyst** at BoscoBoys Distributors, a family-owned logistics company based in Toronto, Ontario.

Built with [React 18](https://react.dev) and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com).

## Overview

A single-page report covering:

- **Company Profile** — BoscoBoys Distributors and the logistics industry
- **Job Description** — Role responsibilities, skills, and projects shipped
- **Learning Goals** — Five co-op learning goals with action plans and reflections
- **Conclusion** — Key learnings and what comes next
- **Acknowledgements** — The people who made the term possible
